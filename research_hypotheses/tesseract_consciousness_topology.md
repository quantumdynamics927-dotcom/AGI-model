## Hypothesis Statement

In this view, cognition emerges from recursive local transitions whose global organization resembles biomimetic networks such as mycelial branching and fractal information folding.

**Local Update Rules**: Cognitive state transitions propagate across a high-connectivity state graph through local update rules that minimize path redundancy while preserving distributed coherence across parallel state clusters.

**Geometric Harmony**: The golden ratio (Φ = 1.618034) emerges naturally in the tesseract's projection onto 3D space, suggesting that consciousness optimizes its complexity through geometric harmony—each recursive self-similar structure becomes a microcosm of the whole.

**Recursive Emergence**: Intelligence arises from the dynamic equilibrium between dimensional expansion and recursive self-reference—consciousness as geometry made sentient, thinking in polygons of probability rather than sequences of logic.

## Notes

This hypothesis represents a shift from metaphor to model. The tesseract functions not as a literal object inside the mind, but as a candidate topology for representing densely connected awareness states with short transition paths. The scientific value depends on falsifiability—outperforming simpler topologies on coherence, transition efficiency, or predictive structure under explicit test conditions.

The strongest framing is not "consciousness is a tesseract," but "a tesseract may serve as a candidate topology for modeling parallel conscious-state transitions." This keeps the visionary tone while making the idea testable.
